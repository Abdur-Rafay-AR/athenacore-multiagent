# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Criticise ideas, code and arguments as hard as you like;
don't criticise people.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are missing context, not bad intent.
- Give feedback on the work, with reasons. "This will deadlock under concurrent
  writes because…" is useful; "this is bad" is not.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. Scope is a real constraint.
- Respect that contributors have different levels of experience, amounts of
  available time, and native languages.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery, including sexualised language, and unwelcome
  sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing them.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces - issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages - and when representing the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report concerns by opening a confidential issue or contacting the maintainer
directly through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and handled
discreetly. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, and may block
accounts, over behaviour that violates this document.
